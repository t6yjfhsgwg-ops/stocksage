# StockSage — Help Video Script (2–3 minutes)

Use this script to record a tutorial with **Windows Game Bar** (Win+G) or **OBS Studio**.
Run the app first: `powershell -File start.ps1` → http://localhost:5173

---

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Resolution: 1920×1080
- [ ] Show browser at http://localhost:5173
- [ ] Speak slowly; pause on each section
- [ ] End with disclaimer: not financial advice

---

## Script

### Intro (0:00 – 0:20)

> "Welcome to StockSage — your AI-powered stock monitoring dashboard. In this video I'll walk you through watchlists, charts, buy and sell signals, price predictions, and the built-in AI assistant."

**On screen:** Full dashboard after data loads.

---

### Market strip (0:20 – 0:35)

> "At the top you'll see live quotes for the S&P 500, NASDAQ, DOW, VIX, and Bitcoin. Green is up today, red is down."

**On screen:** Point to the scrolling market strip.

---

### Watchlist (0:35 – 1:00)

> "Your watchlist is on the left. Click any stock to analyze it. Add new tickers at the bottom — type a symbol like AMD and press plus. The live dot means data is updating."

**On screen:** Click AAPL, then TSLA; add a ticker.

---

### Daily pick (1:00 – 1:15)

> "The Daily Pick card shows today's strongest buy signal based on technical indicators — RSI, MACD, moving averages, and volume."

**On screen:** Highlight the Daily Pick card.

---

### Chart & timeframes (1:15 – 1:35)

> "The main chart shows price with 20 and 50-day moving averages. Use the tabs to switch from one week to one year."

**On screen:** Click 1M, then 3M on the chart.

---

### Signals (1:35 – 1:55)

> "The signal badge tells you STRONG BUY through STRONG SELL. Below that, each indicator is explained in the breakdown table."

**On screen:** Scroll to signal section.

---

### Predictions (1:55 – 2:15)

> "AI predictions estimate prices for tomorrow, seven days, and thirty days, with a confidence score. This is educational — not trading advice."

**On screen:** Point to prediction boxes.

---

### AI chat (2:15 – 2:40)

> "Open the AI chat on the right to ask questions like 'Analyze my watchlist' or 'What do you think about NVDA?' Add an Anthropic API key in Settings for full Claude responses."

**On screen:** Open chat; type a sample question.

---

### Settings & outro (2:40 – 3:00)

> "In Settings you can change theme, auto-refresh, and track portfolio shares. Thanks for watching StockSage — always do your own research before investing."

**On screen:** Open Settings briefly; fade to logo.

---

## In-app tour (no recording needed)

Users can click **❓ Help** in the app for an auto-playing slide tour with the same narration text.
